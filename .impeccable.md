## Design Context
Audience: homeowners exploring a fictional home-maintenance business; Upwork buyers assessing WordPress/Elementor delivery.
Jobs: compare services, see the process and try a non-sending enquiry. Editors must change actual Elementor widgets.
Brand words: practical, neighbourly, precise. Light paper, ink blue, warm yellow; Barlow Condensed with Barlow. Service-manual typography, generous space, honest copy. No invented testimonials or achievements.
Principles: usable without animation; obvious demonstration disclosure; plain service scope; editable content; no external form delivery.
